== Description ==

**Streamlined Payment Solutions**

Experience seamless account-to-account transactions with Fena Payments for the Fena Business Toolkit, enabling your customers to make direct bank transfers with ease. Additionally, benefit from reduced payment processing expenses

Upon activating Fena Payments, featuring Open Banking technology within your WooCommerce checkout, you'll unlock these advantageous functionalities:

* Instant settlement – Enjoy the convenience of having transaction funds promptly deposited into your account.
* Save on fees – Fena offers a affordable solution with simple subscription billing; our Payments only subscription starts from £4.99 +VAT. 
* Bank-level security – Employ bank-grade protection for all transactions conducted over the faster payments network.

**Effortlessly Oversee Transactions via Fena Payments**

Fena Payments simplifies the management of all your transactions, including those from WooCommerce.

Within our Toolkit Portal, you have the ability to:
* Access detailed information on e-commerce payments as well as other financial transactions
* Handle banking details for straightforward transfers across all your orders.
* Incorporate new services and enable your plugins and integrations effortlessly.

**Flexible Pricing Options**

**About Fena**

Fena represents an alternative payment method to traditional debit and credit card payments, facilitating direct, instant bank transfers for businesses through websites, payment links, invoice payments, and QR codes.

Fena is fully regulated by the FCA. Here is a link to the [FCA register](https://register.fca.org.uk/s/firm?id=0014G00002YtfohQAB "FCA register")
